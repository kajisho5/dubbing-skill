# Roadmap — dubbing-skill

Part of the [AI Video Production OS](https://github.com/kajisho5/AI-video-production-OS/blob/main/docs/ecosystem/ROADMAP.md) ecosystem: department **音 (Audio)**, status `active`.
Written from a read-only audit of this repository on 2026-10-30. Each step below is meant to be one pull request; tick it off by linking the PR.

## Where it stands

0.2.1, no release tag yet. 0.1.0: NARRATE ([#4](https://github.com/kajisho5/dubbing-skill/pull/4)), DUB ([#7](https://github.com/kajisho5/dubbing-skill/pull/7)) and AUDIO_DESCRIPTION ([#9](https://github.com/kajisho5/dubbing-skill/pull/9)) are experimental, with all four engines synthesising ([#5](https://github.com/kajisho5/dubbing-skill/pull/5), [#10](https://github.com/kajisho5/dubbing-skill/pull/10)) and the consent gate for voice cloning ([#8](https://github.com/kajisho5/dubbing-skill/pull/8)). Skeleton ([#3](https://github.com/kajisho5/dubbing-skill/pull/3)): `contract` / `doctor` / `engines`, an engine registry whose EngineSpec carries licence, credit and watermark, and a worker. ADR-001 checked the licences of the engines from ecosystem.json / OS ADR-012 (code and weights): Chatterbox Multilingual V3 (default, MIT, watermarked; its Japanese path pulls in GPL pykakasi, so it gets its own venv), VOICEVOX (credit per voice), Style-Bert-VITS2 (AGPL, separate process), Kokoro-82M. ADR-003: translation is a caller input, and no engine is chosen. Cloning a real voice needs recorded consent.

## Wiring

ffmpeg-skill probe, join (NARRATE), audio (extract, `--music --music-volume --gain`, `--replace`) and cut (DUB, AUDIO_DESCRIPTION), silence `--list` (AUDIO_DESCRIPTION gaps). Localise chain (step 11, ADR-014): subtitle-skill `translate` before DUB and `mux` after it, run by the caller on one cue list; DUB and `fit --rate` hand back a length budget per line that does not fit. Expected: loudness; inputs from transcription. Wired in the agent as `speech_dub` (video-production-agent#97, its ADR-085).

Localise in one run: translate → dub → subtitles, speech rate fitted** — the chain as one plan: source cues (transcription, or transcription-skill step 8's script alignment) → subtitle-skill `translate` → DUB → the translated cues as a soft track through subtitle-skill `mux`, with the timing of all three kept to one cue list. Skill side: `dubbing fit --rate` measures each synthesised line and the voice's speaking rate, and reports per cue whether it fits at tempo ≤ `--max-tempo`; for each line that does not, it reports a length budget in the target language (characters, and morae for Japanese), so the caller can ask `translate` for a shorter line instead of speeding the voice up. Agent side (video-production-agent): run the chain, re-ask the translation once with the budget, and report what still does not fit. Rendering any tempo other than 1.0 still waits for audio-only tempo in ffmpeg-skill (Velisa ROADMAP, proposals). 参考：pyvideotrans（考え方のみ。コードは使わない） ([#16](https://github.com/kajisho5/dubbing-skill/pull/16), [docs/decisions.md](docs/decisions.md) ADR-014)

  - Done when: on an en→ja fixture every line either fits its cue or is reported with its budget; the dubbed video, its ja soft track and the cue list share the same times; the consent gate (step 6) still applies to a cloned voice
  - Done (ADR-014): `dubbing fit --rate --lang ja` measures the voice's rate on the clips and gives each line that does not fit a budget in characters and morae, at `--max-tempo` and at tempo 1.0; DUB reports the same budget at tempo 1.0 for every line it cannot place; on the en→ja fixture through the real subtitle-skill `translate` and `mux`, every line fits or has its budget, the re-asked line fits, and the ja soft track has the cue list's times; the consent gate still refuses a cloned voice without its record. Field review 2026-11-05 (Velisa ROADMAP, third round) ([#17](https://github.com/kajisho5/dubbing-skill/pull/17), [#21](https://github.com/kajisho5/dubbing-skill/pull/21), [docs/decisions.md](docs/decisions.md) ADR-015)

VOICEVOX takes it through its user dictionary (added for the run, removed after it), Kokoro as English phonemes, Chatterbox and Style-Bert-VITS2 as respellings. `dubbing terms` lists the terms each line contains, and `dubbing terms --transcript` checks a transcription-skill Transcript of the output for each term read as the dictionary says (kana, or a homophone read with SudachiDict). In CI the real VOICEVOX narrates the three-term medical fixture; transcription-skill (faster-whisper base and small, no prompt; a term is heard when either model hears it) hears all three terms as the dictionary reads them (航空, 福空, 調付). A control run without the dictionary is heard as 高校 and 復興 for 口腔 and 腹腔, so the check tells the readings apart.
